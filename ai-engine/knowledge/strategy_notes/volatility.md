# Volatility Filters and Risk Scaling

Volatility is time-varying and tends to cluster. A strategy can use realized or forecast volatility as a conditioning variable rather than assuming constant risk. Volatility filters can reduce exposure during turbulent periods, while inverse-volatility position sizing can target a more stable risk budget. These techniques can also reduce returns if they de-risk immediately before a rebound, so they must be tested across market regimes and with realistic trading costs.
