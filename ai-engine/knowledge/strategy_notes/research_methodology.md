# Quant Research Methodology

A credible quantitative hypothesis must be falsifiable and evaluated without look-ahead bias or data leakage. Separate development data from out-of-sample evaluation. Include transaction costs and slippage. Compare against a benchmark and examine parameter sensitivity. Walk-forward validation repeatedly fits or selects parameters on historical windows and evaluates them on subsequent unseen windows. A strategy should not be accepted solely because of a high in-sample Sharpe ratio.
